# City of Sierra Madre — Public Information Application

**Client:** City of Sierra Madre, California
**Role:** Power Platform Developer
**Type:** Proof-of-concept demo → converted into a delivered project
**Stack:** Power Pages, Power Automate, Dataverse

## Overview

A public-facing information portal built for the City of Sierra Madre, allowing residents to access city information and services online. Started as a proof-of-concept demo for the city and was converted into an active delivered project after approval.

## My contribution

- Built the **Power Pages** front-end — public-facing pages, forms, and data displays connected to Dataverse
- Built **Power Automate flows** to handle form submissions, notifications, and backend data processing triggered by resident interactions on the portal

`Power Pages` `Power Automate` `Dataverse` `Public Sector / Government Portal Development`
