# Employee Lifecycle Management System — Power Platform Case Study

**Client:** A US state government health department (generalized — details kept confidential per client agreement)
**Role:** Power Platform Developer — configuration, automation, and QA
**Duration:** 1.5+ years (ongoing)
**Stack:** Dynamics 365 CRM, Model-Driven Apps, Power Automate, Dataverse

## Overview

An enterprise employee management system built on Microsoft Power Platform to manage the full employee lifecycle for a large government health department — covering **recruitment, transfers, promotions, and job creation** workflows across multiple departments and approval chains.

## What the system handles

- **Recruitment** — candidate intake, position matching, hiring workflow tracking from requisition to offer
- **Transfers** — inter-department transfer requests, approval routing, effective-date tracking
- **Promotions** — eligibility tracking, approval chains, position/grade updates
- **Job creation** — new position requisitions, budget/headcount linkage, job posting workflow

## My contribution

- **App configuration** — designed and built model-driven app entities, forms, and views for recruitment, transfer, promotion, and job-creation records; configured business rules and field-level validation
- **Process automation** — built Power Automate flows to automate status transitions, approval routing, and notification emails as records moved through each stage (e.g. requisition → review → approval → hire)
- **QA / UAT** — wrote and executed test cases against business requirements, validated end-to-end workflows across each module, identified and documented defects, and supported user acceptance testing cycles ahead of releases
